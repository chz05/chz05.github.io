Hi there! I am an incoming Ph.D. student at UC Riverside, starting in Fall 2026. I am advised by Prof. Qian Zhang and Prof. Elaheh Sadredini. My current research sits around computer architecture and machine learning systems, particularly LLM inference acceleration and AI for chip design.

Before my Ph.D., I was a master's student at **Columbia University**. I also received my B.S. in Computer Science from **UC San Diego** in 2023.

I also work closely with **Prof. Yufei Ding** at UCSD.
